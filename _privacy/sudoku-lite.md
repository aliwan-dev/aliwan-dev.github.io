Thank you for playing **Sudoku: Train Your Mind** (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and strive to collect only minimal data necessary to provide a smooth gaming experience.

* **Locally Stored Data:** Game progress, high scores, puzzle statistics, and game preferences (such as sound and vibration settings) are stored locally on your device.
* **Device Information:** Non-identifiable technical information, such as your device model, operating system version, and performance logs, may be collected automatically to diagnose technical issues and optimize performance.
* **Advertising and Analytics:** If the game displays third-party advertisements or utilizes analytics services (e.g., Google AdMob, Firebase), these third-party providers may collect anonymized advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads and track app stability.

### 2. How We Use Information

The collected data is used exclusively to:

* Maintain, support, and improve the game experience.
* Save your game progress and puzzle completion records.
* Monitor app crashes, errors, and performance metrics.
* Display non-intrusive advertisements (if applicable).
