Your privacy is a priority to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how **Yarn Pop: Bubble Shooter** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information, what data is processed, and your rights regarding your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
**We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).**  
You can play **Yarn Pop: Bubble Shooter** without creating an account, registering, or providing personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment credentials.

### B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
To deliver a smooth and persistent gaming experience, the app stores game data locally on your device (using standard Android local storage):
- **Game Progress & Unlocks**: Level progression, stars earned, and journey map milestones.
- **Player Statistics**: Lifetime match statistics (shots fired, combos made, bubbles popped).
- **Settings & Preferences**: Audio (music/SFX toggles) and haptic feedback preferences.

*Note: All locally stored gameplay data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any external or cloud servers.*

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
The game is designed to function fully offline. We do not operate tracking tools, behavioral profiling systems, or advertising identifiers. However, like most applications distributed via the Google Play Store, standard anonymous technical metrics (such as crash logs, ANR reports, and app integrity verification) may be processed directly by **Google Play Services** in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 2. How We Use Information

Any data processed by the app is used solely to:
- Enable core gameplay mechanics and progression.
- Save and restore your level progress, high scores, and audio preferences.
- Ensure app stability and performance across different Android devices.

**We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.**

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage**: All gameplay saves and configurations reside within your device's private sandbox storage.
- **Data Retention**: Your progress data is retained on your device for as long as the app remains installed. You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by clearing the app data via your device settings (*Settings > Apps > Yarn Pop: Bubble Shooter > Storage > Clear Data*) or by uninstalling the app.

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

**Yarn Pop: Bubble Shooter** is a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has inadvertently provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes standard Google Play Core runtime libraries to manage application delivery and feature integrity. We encourage you to review their privacy practices:
- [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA in California), you have rights regarding your personal information:
- The right to know whether any personal data is being processed (as stated above, we collect no personal data).
- The right to delete your data at any time by clearing app cache/storage or uninstalling the app.
- The right to contact us with any questions or requests regarding your privacy.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the application following any changes signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.
