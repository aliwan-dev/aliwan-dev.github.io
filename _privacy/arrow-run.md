Your privacy is our priority. This document explains what data is collected 
by Arrow Run, how it is used, and your rights regarding your information.

## Data We Collect

### Data You Provide Directly

- Arrow Run does not require account registration or login. We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or financial details. All game features are accessible immediately without providing any personal data.

### Data Collected Automatically

- **We do not collect any data automatically.**
- This application runs completely offline and standalone on your device. We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or automated telemetry data collection tools.

## How We Use Data

Because we do not collect personal data, none of your information is used for tracking, profiling, advertising, or commercial purposes.

Any local data generated is used solely to:
- Provide core game functionality (such as saving completed level progress, the last played level, and user preferences like haptic vibration feedback and reduced motion mode).

**We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.**

## Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage**: All gameplay progress and preference settings are stored locally on your device (using standard on-device storage / SharedPreferences).
- No data is ever transmitted, synchronized, or backed up to external servers or cloud services.
- You can clear all saved application data at any time directly through your device settings ("Clear Storage / Data") or by uninstalling the application.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Retain full control over all gameplay progress and preferences stored locally on your device.
- Delete your saved game data and preferences at any time.

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address below.

## Policy Changes

If there are any significant changes to this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page and/or notify you through an application update.
