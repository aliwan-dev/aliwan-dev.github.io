Your privacy is our priority. This document explains what information is collected by Carrom Pool, how it is used, and the rights you have regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- Carrom Pool does not require any account creation, login, or registration. We do not collect or request any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or user-generated content.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **We do not collect personal data automatically.** 
- The game runs locally on your device and does not track your location, unique device identifiers, browsing activity, or biometric data.
- No third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs are integrated into this application.

## How We Use Information

The application functions solely as an offline board game. Any data processed (such as game preferences, sound effects settings, and match states) is used exclusively to:

- Provide core gameplay functionality and sound effects
- Maintain local user settings during gameplay

**We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.**

## Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage**: All gameplay preferences and configurations are stored strictly on your local device.
- **No Cloud Transmission**: No personal data or gameplay history is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party platforms.
- You can permanently remove all local application data at any time by clearing the app data/cache in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

## Children's Privacy

Carrom Pool is designed to be family-friendly and safe for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age limit in your jurisdiction).

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store personal data on any server:

- You have complete control over your data directly on your device.
- You can delete all locally saved preferences and match data at any time via your device settings.

To exercise any questions or concerns regarding privacy, feel free to contact us at the email address provided below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
