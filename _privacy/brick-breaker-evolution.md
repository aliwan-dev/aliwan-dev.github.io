# Privacy Policy — Brick Breaker Evolution

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Brick Breaker Evolution stores, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

Brick Breaker Evolution does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, contact details, or user-created content.

### Information Stored Automatically

The app stores gameplay progress and preferences on your device. This may include your scores, level progress, stars, upgrades, achievements, selected ball type, sound setting, and daily challenge progress.

The app uses your device’s current date locally to generate the daily challenge and track your streak. It does not send this date or your gameplay information to us.

The current app does not include advertising, analytics, account sign-in, or crash-reporting services, and does not send gameplay or personal information to a developer-operated server.

Android backup may include the app’s locally stored data if backup is enabled on your device. Android manages that backup under your device and Google Account settings.

## How We Use Information

Locally stored information is used only to:

- Save and restore your game progress and preferences
- Calculate achievements, upgrades, and daily challenge progress
- Provide the app’s gameplay features

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for commercial purposes. Android may handle app data through its backup service if you have enabled that feature.

## Storage and Data Security

Gameplay data and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not apply separate encryption to these preferences; their protection relies on Android’s app storage and device security.

Your local data remains on the device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. If Android backup is enabled, backed-up data may be restored when the app is reinstalled. You can manage Android backup through your device settings.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- View your saved progress and preferences within the app
- Delete locally stored data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it
- Manage or disable Android backup through your device settings

We do not keep gameplay records on our servers, so we cannot retrieve or delete server-side gameplay data. For privacy questions, you can contact us using the details below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. We will update the date above and publish the revised policy at this page. For significant changes, we will provide notice through the app or another appropriate channel at least 7 days before the changes take effect.
