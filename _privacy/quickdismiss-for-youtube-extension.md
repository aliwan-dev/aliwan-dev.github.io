Your privacy is our priority. This document explains what data is collected by QuickDismiss for YouTube™, how it is used, and the rights you have.

## Data We Collect

### Data You Provide Directly

- **Extension Preferences**: Configuration choices you make in the extension's Options page, such as your preferred button position (e.g., top-left, top-right, bottom-left), surface display toggles (Home feed, Shorts, Related videos), animation toggle, and undo toast visibility.

### Data Collected Automatically

- **Local Dismiss History (Optional)**: If you choose to enable the "Keep a local history of dismissed videos" feature, the extension records the video title, channel name, video URL, confirmation status, and timestamp of videos you dismiss.
- **No Personal Information**: We do **not** collect your name, email address, Google account credentials, IP address, device identifiers, or any overall browsing history.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do **not** collect diagnostic telemetry, tracking cookies, or analytics.

## How We Use Data

Data is used solely to:

- Provide the core functionality of the extension (applying your custom button placement, remembering interface toggles, and enabling the one-click dismiss and undo actions).
- Display your recent dismissal history locally within the extension's Options page, if enabled.

**We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with any third parties for commercial, advertising, or marketing purposes.**

## Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage Only**: All preferences and history are stored strictly on your device using Chrome's native storage APIs (`chrome.storage.sync` and `chrome.storage.local`).
- **No External Servers**: The extension does not transmit any data to external servers, cloud databases, or developer-hosted endpoints.
- **Browser-Level Security**: Your stored data is protected by Google Chrome's built-in application sandboxing and profile encryption.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access Your Data**: View your stored settings and local dismissal history at any time through the extension's Options page.
- **Delete Your Data**: Clear your entire dismissal history at any time by clicking the "Clear history" button on the Options page.
- **Complete Erasure**: Uninstalling the extension from your browser instantly and permanently removes all local storage and settings associated with it.

To exercise any questions or inquiries regarding your data, contact us at the email address below.

## Policy Changes

If any significant modifications are made to this privacy policy, we will provide notice through extension release notes or an in-browser notification at least 7 days before changes take effect.
