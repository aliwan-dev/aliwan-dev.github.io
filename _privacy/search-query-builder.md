Your privacy is our utmost priority. This document explains what data is collected by **Advanced Search Builder**, how that data is used, and the rights and controls you have over your information.

## Data We Collect

### Data You Provide Directly

- **Search Keywords & Filter Inputs**: When you enter search terms or configure filter parameters (such as `site:`, `filetype:`, `intitle:`, date ranges, etc.), these values are processed locally in your browser purely to construct your desired search query string.
- **Custom Search Presets**: When you save custom presets, the preset name and associated filter configuration are stored to allow quick reuse.

### Data Collected Automatically

- **Recent Search History**: Constructed queries and search URLs are stored locally on your device via `chrome.storage.local` to enable the "Recent Searches" history feature.
- **Domain Autocomplete Suggestions**: If browsing history access is available, recently visited domain names are read in-memory solely to suggest domains in the `site:` input field. This data is never stored, logged, or transmitted.
- **Zero Analytics or Tracking**: **We do not automatically collect personal data, telemetry, crash reports, unique device identifiers, or analytics.**

## How We Use Your Data

All data processed by the extension is used solely to:

- Provide the core visual search query builder functionality.
- Open search results in your chosen search engine (Google, Bing, or DuckDuckGo).
- Save, display, and manage your search history and custom presets.

**We do not sell, rent, monetize, share, or transmit your data to any third parties or advertisers.**

## Data Storage & Security

- **100% Client-Side Storage**: All your presets and history are stored within Chrome's built-in sandboxed storage (`chrome.storage.local` for search history and `chrome.storage.sync` for synchronizing custom presets across your signed-in Chrome devices).
- **No External Servers**: Advanced Search Builder operates entirely within your browser. We do not operate external web servers, databases, or cloud tracking endpoints. Network traffic occurs only when you explicitly submit a search to your chosen search engine.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data at all times:

- **Delete Search History**: You can delete individual search history entries or wipe your entire history at any time using the "Clear all" button in the History tab.
- **Manage Presets**: You can view, edit, or delete any custom presets directly within the Presets tab.
- **Export & Backup**: You can export all your saved presets to a JSON file on your computer at any time via the "Export JSON" feature.
- **Complete Data Removal**: Uninstalling the extension from your browser immediately purges all locally stored data.

If you have questions regarding your rights, please reach out using the contact information below.
